# Shadows

Shadows provide depth and elevation, establishing a visual hierarchy and drawing focus to important elements.

## Scale
- `shadow-sm`: Very subtle shadow for small interactive elements or inputs.
- `shadow-md`: Default elevation for cards and standard dropdowns.
- `shadow-lg`: Prominent shadow for modals, popovers, and elevated navigation bars.
- `shadow-xl`: Deep shadow for floating action buttons or critical alerts.
- `shadow-inner`: Inset shadow for depressed states or specific input styles.
- `shadow-none`: Removes any shadow.

## Themes
- **Light Mode**: Shadows are typically darker and slightly more opaque.
- **Dark Mode**: Shadows are much darker, and often we rely more on subtle borders rather than shadows to define edges in dark environments.
